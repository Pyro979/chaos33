2–4 PLAYERS / 18 CARDS

Fate Ends All Roads

The world didn’t end. It just stopped working.

Setup

Shuffle. For 2 players, deal 7 each and set 4 aside unseen. For 3 players, deal 6 each. For 4 players, deal 4 each and set 2 aside unseen. Choose a starting player to be Fate.

Send a threat

Fate plays any card from their hand as a Threat against the next player clockwise. That player must play a Resource of the same type if able. Compare values:

SACRIFICE · Lower
Discard both cards.

COUNTER · Equal or higher
Choose BURN: discard both cards.
Or TURN: discard the Threat and give the Resource card to the original Fate, who adds it to their hand.

After either a Sacrifice or a Counter, the defending player becomes Fate.

Ricochet

If a player has no matching Resource, pass the Threat clockwise until someone can defend. If it returns to the original Fate, discard the Threat. That player remains Fate and plays another Threat if able. In a 2-player game, a missed defense immediately returns the Threat to Fate.

Winning

After each Threat is fully resolved, check everyone’s hands. Anyone with no cards wins. Multiple players may win together.

Read the road

Top = Threat. Bottom = Resource. Full ink uses yellow for Threat and green for Resource; low ink uses white panels with yellow and green border accents. The number is strength. Square = BODY, circle = WITS, triangle = GEAR. The Resource and Threat types must match. Each card’s upper-right index repeats both halves.
